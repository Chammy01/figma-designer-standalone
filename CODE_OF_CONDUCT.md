# Code of Conduct

Participants are expected to be respectful, constructive, and welcoming across backgrounds and experience levels. Discuss ideas and code, accept good-faith feedback, and respect privacy.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, sexualized conduct, personal attacks, and publishing private information without consent are unacceptable.

Maintainers may remove harmful content, limit participation, or ban repeat offenders, applying proportionate action consistently. Report conduct concerns privately to the repository owner through an agreed private channel; do not post sensitive reports in public issues. A dedicated private reporting channel must be configured before public launch. No contact address is invented by this candidate.
